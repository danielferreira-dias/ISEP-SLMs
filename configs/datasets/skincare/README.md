## Local status

`data/` contains the gated Hugging Face snapshot for
`yuhos16/SkinCaRe`, revision
`6c158a139c31c7ced7a1b1376930f340825577e0`.

The verified local payload contains:

- 4,000 SkinCAP images and 4,000 metadata rows, plus 346 images retained under
  SkinCAP's `not_include/` directory;
- 3,041 SkinCoT images;
- exactly matching sets of 3,041 English and 3,041 Chinese SkinCoT text files.

Access is tied to the Hugging Face account already authenticated on this
machine. Do not redistribute the payload.

Sources:

- <https://huggingface.co/datasets/yuhos16/SkinCaRe>

## Dataset

SkinCaRe contains 7,041 cases in two components:

- `SkinCAP`: 4,000 image-caption records inherited from SkinCAP.
- `SkinCoT`: 3,041 DermNet-derived image-text records with English and Chinese
  hierarchical diagnostic reasoning.

SkinCoT covers broad categories such as eczema, acne/rosacea, psoriasis and
lichen-planus-related conditions, fungal infections, viral infections,
benign tumours, and malignant/precancerous lesions.

The local SkinCoT layout contains 23 category directories. Examples include
`Eczema Photos`, `Acne and Rosacea Photos`,
`Melanoma Skin Cancer Nevi and Moles`, and
`Actinic Keratosis Basal Cell Carcinoma and other Malignant Lesions`.

## Labels and targets

SkinCAP records provide `disease`, `caption_en`, `caption_zh`, image source,
skin-tone fields, coarse diagnostic partitions, and morphology concepts. The
older CSV names its polished English caption field
`caption_zh_polish_en`. Its 4,000 rows contain 187 distinct non-empty disease
strings; normalize aliases before class-based training.

SkinCoT records provide a broad category through their directory, an
image-derived filename, and clinician-reviewed hierarchical narratives in
English and Chinese. The 23 folder names are broad targets, not fine-grained
disease labels. Filenames often encode a more specific condition, and the
narrative states a final diagnosis, but this snapshot has no independent
structured fine-disease column for SkinCoT.

The paper describes reviewer assessment on accuracy, safety, medical
grounding, clinical coverage, reasoning coherence, and description precision.
The downloaded release does not include a per-case numeric review-score table,
so those criteria cannot be used as training labels directly.

A reasoning target follows the conceptual structure:

```text
broad disease family -> observed supporting evidence -> final diagnosis
```

## Training caution

The initial SkinCoT reasoning was generated by an LLM using the known target
label and then reviewed by dermatologists. It can therefore contain
label-conditioned rationalization and generation-style artifacts. Prefer
extracting short auditable findings, differential candidates, uncertainty,
and final labels instead of training an assistant to expose long
chain-of-thought text.

The Hub metadata, card, paper, gated terms, and upstream image licences expose
inconsistent licence descriptions. Obtain written permission before
publishing adapted data or trained weights.
